Picks up from [round 5](2026-09-24-round5.md) and #233's limiter (8083e13). Played on
<https://dungeonkeeper.tfrey7.com/> once `version.json` read 8083e139a128, with the GOG Dungeon

out to the speakers. Nothing was built or fixed. Pictures are in [2026-09-24-round6/](2026-09-24-round6/).

## The big fight's sound effects: no clipping, and they still sound full (#233 confirmed)

`scripts/prove_limiter.mjs` on the live page (level 3, 12 trolls and 8 bile demons against 12
knights and 12 wizards at the heart, 25 s) passed. Effects peaked at 0.848 with **no samples over
full scale** (0 of 1,005 windows). Round 5 had a peak of 1.285 and 751 samples over. Music peaked
at 0.086.

The same fight was then played again with a meter before and after the limiter
(`level3-fight-limiter.png`, 8 s quiet, then 45 s of fight):

| Effects | Into the limiter | To the speakers |
|---|---|---|
| Quiet, before the fight | peak 0.296, -24.69 dB RMS | peak 0.296, -24.69 dB RMS (unchanged) |
| The fight | peak 1.444, 1,826 samples over | peak 0.843, none over |
| Loudness over the whole run | -13.31 dB RMS | -14.13 dB RMS |
| Brightness (share of energy in sample-to-sample change) | 0.0110 | 0.0113 |

Over the fight's 21 ms blocks, the median level fell 0.3 dB. A third of the blocks fell more
than 1 dB and 5.5% more than 3 dB; the most was 7.2 dB, on the loudest hits. Sound under the
threshold passes untouched, the fight is under 1 dB quieter overall, and the high end is kept.
**Nothing is quieter or muffled beyond the peaks the limiter exists to hold**, as a
limiter on the desktop's OpenAL Soft would do. Music went through unchanged (peak 0.305 before and
after). Nobody listened with ears, which headless Chrome cannot do.

## The Options menu's sound-effects slider silences the effects

Escape, then the speaker icon, opens Sound Options with three sliders: sound effects, music and
voice (`sound-options-fx-zero.png`). With the effects slider dragged fully left:

- **In a fight** (level 4: 6 trolls against 3 knights, 8 s), the effects context peaked at
  **0.000**. After dragging it back (`sound-options-restored.png`), the next fight peaked at 0.661.
- **A fresh level** started with the slider at zero was silent from the first frame.
- In the level where it was moved, a faint pulse carried on at peak 0.05 to 0.06, every 2 s
  (-35.7 dB RMS, down from a 0.28 peak). The voice slider at zero did not change it. The
  engine applies the effects volume when a sound starts (`thing_play_sample` scales loudness by
  `settings.sound_volume` and plays nothing at zero), so a looping sound already playing keeps its
  gain. That is the engine's own code and the desktop runs the same, so it is not filed.

This settles round 5's open note: `!volume.sfx 0` left sounds going for the same reason, sounds
already playing at the time.

## Level 3's rooms, built from the room panel

Level 3 (`-alex -level 3`) was given 60,000 gold (`!player.addgold`), 16 more imps and every room
(`!room.available all 1`). All the digging was done by hand: dig tags dragged in the main view
(a sideways drag tags a wide diamond; a diagonal drag only a thin strip), imps dug and claimed. Then
each room was chosen from the room panel and placed with a click:

- **Shift shows the best-fitting shape** under the cursor, clipped to the claimed floor (8 tiles for
  the Graveyard, 2,400 gold; `level3-graveyard-shift-best-shape.png`). **Ctrl shows a 5x5 square**
  with the tiles that can be built in green (15 tiles, 4,500 gold; `level3-graveyard-ctrl-square.png`).
  Shift+click and Ctrl+click build that shape, and the gold drops by the right amount: Graveyard 8 x
  300, Prison 8 x 225, Torture Chamber 13 x 350 (Ctrl), Temple 9 x 350 (Ctrl). This matches the
  desktop's KeeperFX. In a one-tile gap Shift gives a single tile, as it should.
- **Prison takes prisoners.** With Imprison on (Information panel), 4 archers and 3 thieves (level 1)
  were beaten by 6 trolls and 2 dark mistresses. Imps carried them to the Prison: Lua showed
  `CreatureInPrison` for each. They starved there and rose as the keeper's skeletons, 2 at first and
  6 in the end (`level3-prison-skeletons.png`), as on the desktop.
- **Graveyard takes corpses.** A dead wizard lay in it: the log later reads
  `remove_body_from_graveyard: The dead WIZARD is not in room`, when the room was wrecked under it
  (below). Round 5's `creature_drops_corpse_in_graveyard: ... room won't accept it` never showed. So
  a Graveyard built from the panel takes corpses; round 5's Lua-made one did not.
- **Torture Chamber: in use, but no conversion seen.** The two dark mistresses went there on their own
  (`KinkyTorturing`), as they do on the desktop. No prisoner was put on a device: the prisoners were
  level 1 with 1 health and starved within seconds of being jailed, and two hand pick-ups of a
  walking prisoner missed.
- **Temple: not settled.** A 3x3 Temple was built with Ctrl (`level3-temple-under-attack.png`), and a
  skeleton picked from the creature panel was dropped on it. At that moment the trolls were tearing
  the Temple down.
- **Why the rooms went.** No Lair, Hatchery or Treasure Room had been built first, so the trolls grew
  angry and wrecked every room (`instf_attack_room_slab` in the log, the room count 4, 3, 2, 1, 0).
  That is the game's own rule for unhappy creatures, not the port's. Next round: build Lair, Hatchery
  and Treasure Room first, and make stronger heroes (level 4 or more) so prisoners live long enough
  to torture.

Noted, not filed: after an accidental possession (an imp, "Voagooz") was left with a right-click,
the main view stayed looking **straight down** rather than tilted (`level3-top-down-after-possession.png`).
It is not known which input started the possession. Round 4 left possession five times and did not

The Lua console was used only to read creature states (`!lua` with `GetThingsOfClass`, one short
