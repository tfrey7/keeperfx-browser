# Battle test, 2026-09-24, round 8 (job 238)

Picks up from [round 7](2026-09-24-round7.md) and the movies (ce8b325). Played on
<https://dungeonkeeper.tfrey7.com/> live at ce8b325d82b9 (`version.json`), with the **Steam**
Dungeon Keeper folder (`ldata` holds `intromix.smk`, `outromix.smk`, `Drag.smk`), in headless Chrome
at 1280x1000 driven by mouse and keyboard over the DevTools protocol. Nothing was built or fixed.
Pictures are in [2026-09-24-round8/](2026-09-24-round8/). The run had one hour.

## The intro movie

- **Fresh browser** (new profile, folder given on the files page, a real click on Start): the engine
  page downloaded KeeperFX's 158 MB of data in **60 s**, the engine was running at 62.5 s, and the
  intro's sound began at **70.0 s**, 7.5 s after the engine started (the two splash screens come
  first, as on the desktop). Left alone, the intro played to its end (sound until 185.5 s, about
  115 s of movie, `intro-playing.png`) and the main menu followed.
- **Second start** (data kept in the browser): engine running at 2.9 s, intro sound at **10.3 s**.
- **A click skips it**: the main menu was up within 3 s (`intro-click-skipped.png`). **Escape skips
  it** too (from a start with `-alex`). After either, Start New Game led to the land view with its
  music and the level-1 narration, and the log had no movie error. It carries on cleanly.
- The Steam folder's `Drag.smk` is kept as `ldata/drag.smk`; the files page counted 11 of 13 extras.
- **Filed (#240): opening the game page directly plays the intro in silence.** Opened as a new tab
  or bookmark (`engine.html`, not through Start), the browser has had no click yet, so both audio
  contexts stay `suspended` for the whole intro, and nothing on the page says to click for sound
  (`intro-cold-open-silent.png`). The first click brings sound back, but it also skips the movie.
  A reload after a click on the page plays it with sound. On the desktop the intro always has sound.

## Level 5 (Lushmeadow-on-Down)

Reached the way a player would with cheats on (`engine.html?args=-alex -nointro`): Start New Game,
Ctrl+F10 four times on the land view, level 5's flag. The briefing ("Another Keeper controls this
underworld realm...") shows over a dark, candle-lit heart (`level5-start.png`). Given 50,000 gold
(`!player.addgold 0 50000`) and 16 imps (`!create.creature IMP 1 16 0`).

- Chat commands must be typed key by key: text pasted into the page is not seen by the engine.
  A player types, so this is not a fault.
- Digging by sideways drags and a 5x5 Treasure Room with Ctrl (`level5-treasure-ctrl.png`: 16 tiles,
  800 gold, taken exactly) work as on the desktop. A drag with a room still chosen lays that room
  along the drag, which is also the desktop's behaviour.
- **The Prison.** With every room made available (`!room.available all 1`), a Ctrl-placed Prison
  was built (two blocks, 225 gold a tile, `level5-prison-built.png`). The Imprison button on the
  information panel sparkled as new and, pressed, showed the prison bars (`level5-imprison-on.png`).
  A Knight, the Lord of the Land, was made for the heroes beside it with 4 level-8 trolls of mine
  (`!create.creature KNIGHT 1 1 PLAYER_GOOD`, `!create.creature TROLL 8 4 0`); they beat him and the
  imps carried him to the Prison inside 40 s.

## Level 5 won, and its cut-scene: the Lord's torture movie

Level 5 was won from the cheat menu (F12, "Win level"): the rival keeper was not fought, as there
was not time. The log said `Lord Of The Land kept captive. Torture tower unlocked.` Space carried
on, and **`drag.smk` played** (`FeSt_DRAG`, `drag-movie.png`: the corridor to the TORTURE sign),
exactly where the desktop plays it: after a level won with the Lord captive. It was silent, which is
right: the file has no sound track (its Smacker header lists no audio). It took about 1.5 s to start.
**Escape** skipped it; the torture screen was passed at once too (`FeSt_DRAG` → `FeSt_TORTURE` →
`FeSt_LEVEL_STATS` within 2.5 s of one press), then the Statistics (`level5-statistics.png`), the
level-5 closing narration and the high-score table. Whether one Escape skipping both is also the
desktop's behaviour was not checked, so it is **not filed**; worth a look next round.

The campaign's outro after level 20 was not driven again: job 235's proof shows it on this build.
