KeeperFX

Dungeon Keeper, on the open-source KeeperFX engine, in your browser. It plays with your own copy of the original game.

Looking for your game files…

Where is your Dungeon Keeper?

KeeperFX needs a few files from the original game. Choose the folder you installed Dungeon Keeper (or Dungeon Keeper Gold) into, or a .zip of that folder.

Your files stay on this computer. They are checked and kept in this browser's own storage; nothing is uploaded, ever.

Where do I find it?
GOG
C:\GOG Games\Dungeon Keeper Gold, or with GOG Galaxy C:\Program Files (x86)\GOG Galaxy\Games\Dungeon Keeper Gold. In Galaxy: the game's settings button → Manage installation → Show folder.
Steam
C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Dungeon Keeper. In Steam: right-click the game → Manage → Browse local files.
EA app / Origin
C:\Program Files\EA Games\Dungeon Keeper, or C:\Program Files (x86)\Origin Games\Dungeon Keeper.
CD
The KEEPER folder on the disc.

Choosing the outer folder is fine: the page looks inside it for the data and sound folders it needs.

That folder is missing these files, so nothing was kept:

    Check you chose the folder Dungeon Keeper is installed in, not a shortcut to it. A demo or "Deeper Dungeons" on its own does not have them.
