## Loading KeeperFX's data

Timed from Start on the files page to "The engine is running.", with the browser's resource timings
(GitHub Pages serves the data with `Cache-Control: max-age=600`):

| Load | Time | Data over the wire |
|------|------|--------------------|
| First ever, fresh browser profile | 27.6 s (data from 0.9 s) | 158 MB, 2,156 files |
| Reload 1 min later | 5.6 s | none: 2,155 of 2,156 files from the cache |
| Reload 6 min later | 4.7 s | none |
| Reload 30 min after the first load (cache stale) | 14.6 s | 0.6 MB: all 2,156 files asked again and answered "not modified" |

A first load is 27.6 s here, near round 1's 20 s; round 2's 70 s was not seen. A reload once the
ten-minute cache window has passed costs about 9 s more than a fresh-cache one, because every one of
the 2,156 files is checked with the server (8 at a time, median 31 ms each) though nothing is sent.
The desktop game has no load at all, but this is the cost of any web page's cache, so it is noted
rather than filed. Round 2's 70 s is most likely that same check over a slower connection.

## What worked

- **Level 1's end screens**: Statistics with the debrief, then the High Score Table waiting for a
  name (a cheat win scores 35 and is listed as row 11 below the nine Bullfrog entries, which looks
  like KeeperFX's own layout), then the land view with the good02 briefing.
- **Continue Game** after a page reload returns to the land view with Cosyton next.
- **Level 2 (Cosyton)**: seen from straight above (after a click on the parchment map), dig tags
  painted a 20x7 block south of the heart and a corridor north to the portal, and 6 more imps
  were made; a Treasure Room, Lair and Hatchery were placed; the portal was claimed and a spider and
  a fly came in (`portal-claimed.png`), and the Training Room was offered with its mentor line.
- **Possession, in a calm scene**: a creature was picked up from the creature panel and dropped
  in the empty corridor, and Possess Creature cast straight away on that spot took over an imp
  (`possessed.png`). W walked it down the corridor to the heart, the mouse turned it, a click
  swung, and a right-click gave it back. Round 2's clicks most likely missed moving creatures.
- **Save in level 2**: Options → Save "round3" wrote the game (the log shows the save's Lua data).

## Not reached in the hour

- **Library research**: Cosyton offers no Library (only the Treasure Room, Lair, Hatchery and,
  later, the Training Room). The Library comes with level 3. Round 2 had one only because of the
  "every room" cheat. Watch research in level 3.
- **Winning level 2 and playing level 3**: the heroes had not come when time ran out.

Cheats used (`-alex`): Win level for level 1 (the win itself was proved in round 2), 40,000 gold in
level 2, and `!power.give all`. Possess Creature did not need the cheat: level 2 grants it from the
start as the first spell icon, which round 2 may have missed.
