# Battle test, 2026-09-24, round 4 (job 231)

Picks up from [round 3](2026-09-24-round3.md) and #230's possession fix (52dfe6b). Played on
<https://dungeonkeeper.tfrey7.com/> once it was live with 52dfe6b (`version.json`), with the GOG
Dungeon Keeper Gold folder, in headless Chrome at 1280x1000 driven by mouse and keyboard over the
DevTools protocol (focus emulation on, so the page may take pointer lock). `engine.html?args=-alex`
throughout. Nothing was built or fixed. Pictures are in [2026-09-24-round4/](2026-09-24-round4/),
at the engine's 640x480.

## Possession now holds the mouse (#230 confirmed)

In level 1, with Possess Creature given by cheat and cast on the keeper's creatures in a fight
at the heart:

- **The lock is taken and turning does not stop.** Every possession put `document.pointerLockElement`
  on the canvas. Moving the mouse 1,000 px to the right, far past the edge of the page, kept
  turning the view all the way (`possession-turns.png`: two possessions, start, end, and after
  leaving).
- **Entering and leaving, five times over**, the lock log reads locked canvas / locked nothing in
  strict pairs, by right-click and by Escape alike.
- **Escape and the options.** Escape while possessing leaves the creature, which is what KeeperFX's
  own code does (`get_creature_control_action_inputs` leaves on Escape or right-click), so it is
  not a fault. Shift+Escape opens Options while still possessing (`possession-options.png`): the
  lock stays with the canvas, the menu answers the pointer, and Escape closes it back into the
  creature. (A real Escape key also makes the browser itself drop the lock; the driver's synthetic
  keys do not, so that part was not seen.)
- A click that misses a creature casts nothing and takes no lock, as on the desktop.

## A level lost

Level 1 was lost by letting 15 heroes loose at the heart (`!create.creature 249 4 15 PLAYER_GOOD`):
the heart broke, the view pulled back and "You have been defeated. Press Space to restart." showed
(`defeated.png`); both audio contexts kept running (peaks 0.01 to 0.12). Space went back to the
land view with level 1 to play again, as on the desktop. The log reads
`set_player_as_lost_level: PLAYER0 lost` then `Lost level state selected`.

## Levels 1 to 3

Levels 1 and 2 were won with the cheat menu (F12, Win level) to reach level 3; each went through
Statistics and the High Score Table to the land view with the next flag, as round 3 saw.

**Level 3 (Waterdream Warm)** starts with 3,000 gold, four imps and the mentor asking for a
Treasure Room, Lair, Hatchery and Training Room (`level3-start.png`). The parchment map, zoom by
wheel, Delete to rotate, arrow-key scrolling, dig tags (dragged), Create Imp, Treasure Room and
Lair placement all behaved as on the desktop. With `!room.available all 1` every room became
buildable (`level3-all-rooms.png`: Workshop, Prison, Torture Chamber, Temple, Graveyard, Barracks,
Guard Post, Bridge and the rest; none was built in the time).

## Speed and sound in a big fight

Fifteen trolls, ten bile demons, twelve knights and twelve wizards made at the heart of level 3
(`level3-fight.png`). Frames a second counted with `requestAnimationFrame` over 4 to 5 s:

| Scene | Frames a second | Worst frame |
|---|---|---|
| Level 3, quiet | 238 | 8.4 ms |
| Level 3, the fight in view | 237 to 239 | 8.3 to 12.6 ms |

Headless Chrome is not held to a 60 Hz screen, so these are well above what a player sees; no frame
took longer than 12.6 ms, so there is no stutter at 60 Hz. The game logic is not slowed.

Both of the page's audio contexts ran throughout. One context's peak rose to 0.4 to 1.0 through the
fight (0.5 before, with music), the other held 0.01 to 0.04. A peak of 1.0 (once 1.26 in level 1's
fight) is full scale, so the loudest moments may clip where the desktop's OpenAL Soft limits its
output; the meter could not tell which context is effects and which is music, and nobody listened,
so this is noted, not filed. A person listening to a big fight with headphones would settle it.
