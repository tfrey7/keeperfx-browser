# Battle test, 2026-09-24, round 7 (job 237)

Picks up from [round 6](2026-09-24-round6.md). Played on <https://dungeonkeeper.tfrey7.com/> live at
eb2c7daa56f8 (`version.json`), with the GOG Dungeon Keeper Gold folder, in headless Chrome at
1280x1000 driven by mouse and keyboard over the DevTools protocol. Nothing was built or fixed.
Pictures are in [2026-09-24-round7/](2026-09-24-round7/). The run had one hour; level 4 took all of
it, so level 5 was not started and **no level was won**.

## Level 4 (Flowerhat): its new rooms work as on the desktop

Level 4 was started with `engine.html?args=-alex -level 4`, then given 30,000 gold and 22 more imps
(`!player.addgold`, `!create.creature IMP`).

- **Digging and the room panel.** A sideways drag in the tilted view tagged a wide diamond, and the
  imps dug it out and claimed it. Treasure Room, Lair and Hatchery were placed with Ctrl for a 5x5
  square (`level4-treasure-ctrl-square.png`: 22 tiles buildable, 1,100 gold, and the gold dropped by
  exactly that). Where the square fell partly on unclaimed floor, only the claimed tiles were built.
  One Ctrl placement over broken floor made three separate Lairs (the panel read "x3"). KeeperFX
  does the same on the desktop.
- **The Workshop unlocks when it should.** Once Treasure Room, Lair, Hatchery, Training Room,
  Library and the Portal were all held, the mentor's "A study of dungeon designs reveals that
  Workshops..." played and the Workshop appeared in the room panel, as the level script says. A
  10-tile Workshop was built (200 gold a tile), and the information event for a Workshop of nine
  tiles or more arrived (`level4-workshop-built.png`).
- **Research.** Four warlocks were put in the Library. Bridge and Call to Arms were both researched
  inside three minutes (`level4-bridge-researched.png`). After that the warlocks stood idle: the
  level has nothing more to research.
- **The Workshop made nothing in 20 minutes.** Two to three trolls were counted as working, but no
  door or trap came out, and the log repeated
  `creature_job_in_room_no_longer_possible_f: manufacturing: Room RESEARCH index 10 is not valid
  ROOM_ROLE_CRATES_MANUFACTURE for creature TROLL` about once a minute. The Workshop had been placed
  in two goes next to the Library, which was built on floor made by Lua (below). The same engine runs
  on the desktop, and this layout can't be made there without cheats, so it is **not filed**. It is
  worth one clean try next round: a Workshop built on hand-dug floor, away from the Library.
- **The parchment map** (the book at the top of the panel) opens the whole level ("Flowerhat",
  `level4-map.png`), and a click on it jumps the view there. The view comes back facing north
  instead of the diagonal it had before, still tilted. That is KeeperFX's own behaviour.

## Getting to the heroes

Most of the hour went on reaching the heroes' castle north of the dungeon. Most of the rock between
them is impenetrable, and the veins of earth wind; hovering over the rock gives the "cannot dig
through" tooltip, as on the desktop. When dig tags had no reachable neighbour, all 25 imps stood idle
(`level4-imps-idle.png`); that is the game's own rule. To save time, a corridor north of the heart
was cut with Lua (`ChangeSlabType`/`ChangeSlabOwner`, heart positions from
`PLAYER0.heart.pos.stl_x`/`stl_y`: the keeper's heart is at slab 40,69 and the heroes' heart at 40,33).
A 20-tile strip of claimed floor was also laid for the Library and Training Room. With the corridor
open, the imps dug to the castle in a minute, and reaching action point 2 gave the objective "When
your troops are sufficient in number and have had some training, lead them north and crush any who
oppose you" (`level4-lead-them-north.png`).

After the Lua slab changes the log showed several
`process_creature_state: The creature IMP index ... has illegal state[1], S=0, TCS=0, reset`, and
the imp count fell from 25 to 16. The tiles changed under imps that were already walking on them;
this is not something a player can do, so it is **not filed**.

## The push north: not finished

8 demonspawn (level 5), 4 trolls, 4 warlocks and 4 beetles were made with `!create.creature`.
**Call to Arms** could not be cast on the heroes' own ground (the forbidden cursor showed) but could be
cast on claimed floor at the castle's edge (`level4-call-to-arms.png`). The log printed one
`set_call_to_arms_as_birthing: Invalid CTA object life state 0` error there, but the banner was placed
and creatures walked to it. The demonspawn, picked from the creature panel and dropped on the banner
with a right click, joined the call. The heroes' heart sits in a walled room reached from its north side
(`level4-heart-walled.png`), and the imps were still claiming the castle floor towards it when the hour
ran out. No hero fought back in that time. One fly died.
