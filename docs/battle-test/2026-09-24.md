# Battle test, 2026-09-24 (job 208)

Played on <https://dungeonkeeper.tfrey7.com/> (engine `git:211438f`, KeeperFX 1.4.0.0 web) with a
GOG Dungeon Keeper Gold folder, in headless Chrome driven by mouse and keyboard over the DevTools
protocol, as a player would. Nothing was built or fixed. Screenshots are in this folder, at the
engine's own 640x480.

## What worked

- **Files page**: the folder is accepted, 14 of 14 required files and 10 of 12 optional extras are found, and Start opens the engine; KeeperFX's 158 MB of data loaded in about 20 s.
- **Main menu → land view → Eversmile**: the land view, the Eversmile tooltip, the spoken briefing (`good01.mp3`) and music track 2 all work; level 1 loads with track 3 (`level1-start.png`).
- **Sound**: both audio contexts are running and carry signal (peaks 0.02 to 0.44) from the first click onward. Before any click the browser keeps them suspended, as every browser does.
- **Speed**: the game's own `!fps` reads "Framerate/Turn is 20 fps", which is correct (`turn-rate.png`), and `!turn` confirmed about 20 turns/s at normal speed and about 170 at fast forward x8. The canvas presented 90 to 108 frames/s throughout, with no drop seen, but headless Chrome is not a real display.
- **Mouse and keys**: the wheel zooms, WASD and the arrow keys scroll, Left Ctrl + arrows rotates, H goes to the heart, Ctrl+= and Ctrl+- change fast forward, Escape opens the options, and chat commands can be typed. Right-click is kept from the browser, and neither the wheel nor the keys scroll the page.
- **Map and minimap**: the parchment map opens from the book, a click on it moves the camera there, and a right-click closes it. The minimap follows the camera.
- **Digging and rooms**: dragging tags earth and gold (DK1 paints the tiles you pass over rather than a box). The Treasure Room was built to 9 tiles, which gave "Expertly done" (`treasure-room.png`). The portal was claimed, the gold seam mined, and the Lair and a Hatchery tile were placed.
- **The hand**: an imp picked up from the creature panel dangles from the hand and a right-click drops it on claimed floor (`hand-imp.png`). The hand will not drop on unclaimed floor, as on the desktop.
- **Portal**: three flies and then two beetles came, with the mentor's beetle message (`beetle-arrives.png`).
- **Save and load mid-level**: Options → Save "mid1" reached IndexedDB. Options → Load lists it (`load-menu.png`) and loads it in about 6 s, back to the saved gold and dungeon (`loaded.png`).
- **Quitting a level**: Options → Quit → tick returns to the land view, and Escape there returns to the main menu.

## Not reached in the hour

The level was not won. The tutorial holds the Hatchery until 9,000 gold has been mined, and it
took a while to find that the full treasure room had stopped the imps mining. The Hatchery
arrived at the end of the time box, and the three hero battles, the win, the level-end screens
and level 2 were not reached. Level 1 has no Library, Training Room, research, spells to cast
or creature to possess: its script offers only the hand, slap and Create Imp, so those need
level 2 onward. Job 201 already proved the win itself (`docs/proof/level-won.png`). A follow-up
battle test should start from a save made later in the level.

Things noticed but not filed, because they may be the same on the desktop:
- The tutorial text says "right ctrl" to rotate, but KeeperFX binds Left Ctrl. This comes from upstream.
- The wheel zooms on the middle of the screen, not on the pointer.
