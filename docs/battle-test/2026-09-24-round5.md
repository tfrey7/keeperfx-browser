# Battle test, 2026-09-24, round 5 (job 232)

Picks up from [round 4](2026-09-24-round4.md). Played on <https://dungeonkeeper.tfrey7.com/> live at
4a14cec (`version.json`), with the GOG Dungeon Keeper Gold folder, in headless Chrome at 1280x1000
driven by mouse and keyboard over the DevTools protocol, with every `AudioContext` metered on its way
to the speakers (an analyser between the context's output and its destination, read every 25 ms).
Nothing was built or fixed. Pictures are in [2026-09-24-round5/](2026-09-24-round5/).

## Getting to level 3 with a working dungeon

- Levels 1 and 2 were won with the cheat menu (F12, Win level) at first. **The cheat menu answers
  a left click on a line**, not Enter. KeeperFX's `gui_process_inputs` activates a line on mouse
  release. Enter also opens the chat line, so round 4's "Win level answers Enter" only worked by
  accident. After three failed goes in level 2, level 3 was started directly with
  `engine.html?args=-alex -level 3` (KeeperFX's own `-level` switch). Levels 4 and 5 were started
  the same way.
- Digging and room placement by eye in the tilted view were too slow for one hour. The dungeon was
  laid out with KeeperFX's Lua console instead (`!lua`, `ChangeSlabType`/`ChangeSlabOwner`). That
  gave ten 5x5 rooms north of the heart, joined by a corridor: Library, Treasury, Lair, Hatchery,
  Training Room, Workshop, Prison, Torture Chamber, Temple and Graveyard (`level3-library.png`,
  `level3-rooms-working.png`). The keeper was given 50,000 gold, 16 imps, 4 warlocks, 3 trolls,
  2 bile demons, 2 dark mistresses, 2 skeletons and 3 orcs. The game was saved with
  `!game.save 1 round5`.
- Typing into the chat line: when the last character and Enter arrive in the same frame, the last
  character is lost. A pause of a quarter second before Enter fixes it. Nobody types that fast, so
  this is not filed.
- Changing the heart's own tile with Lua made the engine exit to the home page. Players can't
  do that without cheats, so it is not filed.

## Research, the Workshop and the rooms

- With `!room.available all 1` given up front, the warlocks and trolls sat idle: level 3 then has
  nothing left to research or make. The mentor kept saying "Now would be an excellent time to train
  a Warlock". This is the game's own rule, not the port's.
- Warlocks picked up with the creature panel ("Pick idle creatures") and dropped in the Library
  with a right click stayed idle for the same reason. Once the cheat menu's *All rooms and magic
  researchable*, *All doors manufacturable* and *All traps manufacturable* were on, the panel showed
  3 warlocks and 3 trolls working within a minute. Two workshop events (hammer) and a research event
  (book) arrived on the event bar (`level3-research-panel.png`, `level3-rooms-working.png`).
- Gold fell steadily, 53,000 to 38,000 in about ten minutes of training and paydays.
- In the fight below, imps carried corpses to the Graveyard and it refused them
  (`creature_drops_corpse_in_graveyard: ... room won't accept it`). That Graveyard was made by Lua
  slab changes, not built by hand, so this is not filed. A Graveyard built from the room panel should
  be tried next round.
- Prison, Torture Chamber and Temple were built and reachable. No prisoner was taken and nothing
  was sacrificed in the time.

## Which audio context clips in a big fight: settled

The page has two audio contexts. Their makers were recorded when they were created:

| Context | Made by | Nodes | Turned off by |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | `_alcCreateContext` (Emscripten OpenAL) | ~6,000 buffer sources, 260 panners | (not `!volume.sfx 0`, see below) |
| 1 | an SDL `EM_ASM` block | 1 script processor | `!volume.music 0` (peak fell to 0) |

So context 0 is the **sound effects** and context 1 is the **music**. In level 3, 12 trolls,
8 bile demons, 12 knights and 12 wizards were made at the heart (`level3-fight-clipping.png`) and
metered for 25 s:

| | Effects (0) | Music (1) |
|---|---|---|
| Quiet, 8 s before | peak 0.39, nothing over 1.0 | peak 0.002 |
| The fight, 25 s | **peak 1.285, 751 samples over full scale, 21 of 1,493 windows over 1.0** | peak 0.05 |

**The effects clip in a big fight.** The desktop's OpenAL Soft limits its output; the browser's
OpenAL just adds up the sources. Filed as **#233**.

Not settled: with `!volume.sfx 0` the effects context still peaked at 0.37 while the fight went on.
That may be because the console command only changes some sounds, not all. It was not checked
against the Options menu slider, so it is not filed.
