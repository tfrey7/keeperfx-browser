The follow-up to [round 1](2026-09-24.md), picking up where it stopped. Played on
<https://dungeonkeeper.tfrey7.com/> (KeeperFX 1.4.0.0 web) with a GOG Dungeon Keeper Gold folder, in
headless Chrome at 1280x800 driven by mouse and keyboard over the DevTools protocol. Nothing was
built or fixed. Pictures are in [2026-09-24-round2/](2026-09-24-round2/), at the engine's 640x480.
Headless Chrome's frame rate is not a real display's, so speed is judged by the game's own turn
counter (`!turn`), not by frames.

Cheats (`engine.html?args=-alex`) were used only to skip work round 1 already proved: the tutorial
flag past the 9,000-gold mining step in level 1, gold and every room in level 2. The hero waves,
the win and the level-end screens were played for real.

## Follow-up #226: the debrief is heard

Job 226 went back to the live site to reproduce the silent Statistics screen and could not. Five
routes to it were tried, each winning level 1 and pressing Space: a quick win by cheat with Space
four seconds later, a win under fast forward, a long wait on the won level before Space, the level's
own win script (the Lord of the Land dying), and a won level saved, the page reloaded and the save
loaded from the main menu. The runs were done in headless Chrome, and once more in a visible Chrome
window using the real sound card.

Every time, SDL's audio context read peaks of 0.16 to 0.49 on the Statistics screen: `bad01.mp3`
starts about 2 s after the screen opens and is over by about 10 s, then the screen is quiet, as on
the desktop once the mentor has finished. So the 15 s sample in this round most likely began after
the debrief had ended. Tim closed it as not reproducible, with no code change.

**For the next battle test:** start sampling both audio contexts *the moment the Statistics screen
opens* (as Space is pressed on "Success!"), keep going for at least 12 s, and log the peak for each
second rather than one peak for the whole window. A debrief that really is silent then reads 0 from
2 s to 10 s; one that played shows its peak there and silence after. Note the time from Space to
the first sample alongside the result.

## What worked

- **The page fills the window (#213)**: at 1280x800 the game is scaled to 1008x756 at its own 4:3
  shape under a slim bar with Full screen and Engine log buttons; the log is in a drawer
  (`fills-window.png`).
- **Quitting (#214)**: Options → Quit → tick in a level goes to the land view, Escape to the main
  menu, Quit there closes the game, and the page says "The game has closed." with Play again and
  Your game files (`after-quit.png`).
- **A late level 1 save**: Options → Save "late1" after the Treasure Room, Lair and Hatchery; it
  survived a page reload and was listed and loaded from the main menu's Load Game.
- **Level 1's hero waves and the win**, at normal speed (about 18 to 20 turns a second by `!turn`):
  the tunnellers ("Intruders approach"), the first battle with its Fight panel (`first-battle.png`),
  "Savour your first victory", the Lord of the Land (`lord-arrives.png`), and "Success! The land is
  yours" (`level1-won.png`). The fights themselves happened out of the camera's view.
- **Level-end screens**: Space → Statistics (two pages), Ok → High Score Table with the new entry
  waiting for a name, typed and kept (`high-scores.png`), Ok → the land view with Eversmile taken and
  the next realm flagged (`land-view.png`), music track 2 and the good02 briefing audible.
- **Level 2 (Cosyton)** loads with track 4 and its briefing. Rooms were dug and placed; the
  Training Room and the Library (research) were built (`level2-rooms.png`,
  `training-library.png`); creatures went to train on their own, paying their 15 gold a session.
- **A spell**: Speed Monster from the Research Panel cast on a creature (`speed-cast.png`).
- **Sound in busy scenes**: through the level 1 fights both audio contexts carried signal
  throughout (effects peaks up to 0.48, music 0.01 to 0.15).
- **Fast forward** (Ctrl+= / Ctrl+-) works. It is kept when a save is loaded in the same session,
  which may well be the same on the desktop, so it is not filed.

## Not reached in the hour

- **Possession**: Possess Creature was given by cheat and selected, but four clicks at creatures
  during a heart attack at normal speed did not take one over. Most likely the clicks missed moving
  creatures; this is not filed as a fault and wants a calm scene next time.
- **Research progress** in the Library was not watched to a result, and level 2 was not won.
- The heroes in level 2 (thieves and dwarves) arrived and were attacking the heart when time ran out.

Noticed but not filed: a new page load takes about 70 s to fetch KeeperFX's 158 MB of data again
(round 1 saw 20 s on a first load); worth measuring on a real connection before calling it a fault.
